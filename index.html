STUDENT SCHOOL EXPERIENCE & FINANCIAL COPING SURVEY

Purpose of the Survey

This survey seeks to understand the real experiences of students in school, especially the financial, academic, social, and emotional challenges they face.

We want to understand how students cope with school fees, feeding, transportation, learning materials, personal expenses, and other responsibilities while pursuing their education.

Your honest responses will help us understand the struggles students face, the support systems available to them, and the different ways they have managed to overcome difficult situations.

Your responses should be honest. You may skip any question you are uncomfortable answering.
